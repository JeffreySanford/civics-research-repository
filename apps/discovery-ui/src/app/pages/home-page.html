Architecture

Authority stays explicit. Search stays rebuildable.

Repository content and federated metadata have different owners and lifecycles. The platform keeps those boundaries visible instead of flattening every source into one opaque index.

Curated authority

DSpace 9

System of record for curated research objects, relations, versions, metadata, and bitstreams.

Federated retention

Application PostgreSQL

Reproducible external metadata, harvest checkpoints, quarantine, corpus identity, and evidence history.

Discovery projection

Solr + OpenSearch

Research experience

Engineering evidence

More than a catalog mockup

The repository is designed to make scale, provenance, accessibility, and operational behavior reviewable—not just visually convincing.

Reproducible at scale

Exact corpus composition, Gold Master archives, restart-safe active projection identity, and measured million-record Solr/OpenSearch parity make scale claims repeatable.

Accessible by construction

Keyboard behavior, reflow, zoom, forced colors, browser axe scans, and semantic map equivalents are part of the delivery evidence.

Typed and inspectable
