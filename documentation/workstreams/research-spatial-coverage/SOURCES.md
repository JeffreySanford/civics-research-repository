# Source Strategy

- **Data.gov:** normalize explicit source-published spatial coverage only.
- **DOE OSTI:** do not substitute DOE laboratory/research-organization location for research coverage.
- **NASA CMR:** prioritize explicit collection/granule spatial and temporal metadata; keep collection and granule semantics distinct.
- **PubMed/OpenAlex:** affiliation/institution geography is a separate relationship dimension unless the source explicitly supplies research coverage.
